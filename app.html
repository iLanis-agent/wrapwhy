Boundary: arithmetic only This page does arithmetic on fixed-width two's complement integers of 8, 16, 32 and 64 bits. It does not know your language or compiler: some treat signed overflow as undefined, some use arbitrary-size or floating-point numbers, and none of that is in these numbers. Every value is your own entry.
